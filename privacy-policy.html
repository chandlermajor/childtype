⌨️ ChildType Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

ChildType is a Chrome extension designed to help children (ages 5-12) practice typing and keyboard finger placement. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data Collection

ChildType does NOT collect, upload, or transmit any user data to external servers.

All data is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's chrome.storage.sync API.

Data TypeStorage LocationPurposeUploaded?
Typing settings (layout, font size, theme, sound)chrome.storage.syncPreserve preferencesNo
Practice stats (WPM, accuracy, duration)chrome.storage.syncTrack progressNo
Level & achievement datachrome.storage.syncGamificationNo
Daily practice historychrome.storage.syncStatisticsNo

2. Data Storage

All data is stored in Google Chrome's chrome.storage.sync API:

  • Data syncs with your Google account across devices you've signed into
  • Only you can access your data (tied to your Google account)
  • Google does NOT read, analyze, or use your extension data
  • When offline, data automatically falls back to chrome.storage.local

3. Data Usage

Data is used ONLY for:

  • Saving your practice settings and preferences
  • Tracking and displaying your typing progress
  • Calculating levels and achievements
  • Providing daily/weekly practice statistics

Never used for: ads, user profiling, third-party sharing, analytics, or any commercial purpose.

4. Data Sharing

ChildType does NOT share data with any third parties.

  • No sharing with advertisers
  • No sharing with analytics companies
  • No sharing with other extensions
  • Google only stores sync data (same technology as Google Drive), not for other purposes

5. User Rights

Access Data

View stored data via Chrome Extensions page:

  1. Open chrome://extensions/
  2. Find ChildType
  3. Click "Inspect views" or check extension data

Delete Data

  • In-app: Settings → Reset all data
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling the extension removes all stored data

6. Child Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

ChildType is designed for children aged 5-12. We comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act):

  • No collection of children's personally identifiable information
  • No account registration required
  • No parental consent needed
  • All data stored locally in browser
  • No online interaction or social features

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact: support@childtype.example.com

This privacy policy is written in compliance with Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies and GDPR principles.
